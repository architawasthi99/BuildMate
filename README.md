# 🏡 BuildMate

BuildMate is a website that helps people build their homes. It brings together people who have land to sell builders, material suppliers and ideas for home designs in one place.

## 🚀 Project Overview

When you want to build a house you have to deal with a lot of people. This includes the person who owns the land the builder, the electrician, the plumber, the carpenter, the painter and the person who sells the materials.

BuildMate wants to be a website where you can do all of these things:

* Look at land that is for sale

* Find builders you can trust

* Find people who sell materials

* Look at ideas for home designs

* Talk directly to the people who can help you
